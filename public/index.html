Module

Logistics Management Software

For small businesses, project teams, facilities, manufacturers, 3PLs, and anyone managing materials and property.

  • List of materials intake: upload Excel, CSV, PDF, Word, Google Docs/Sheets, or a photo. The AI extracts it and flags low-confidence fields for your review.
  • Baseline and revisions: lock an approved baseline, record the approver's decision with the signed or emailed approval attached, and keep every version.
  • Variance engine: quantity, cost, part number, substitution, unit of measure, and schedule changes, with dollar and percent impact.
  • Long-lead and delivery estimates: configurable thresholds, labeled as AI estimate, supplier-quoted, confirmed, or manual.
  • Procurement tracking: from quote to stocked, with exception flags and reminders when updates go stale.
  • Inventory and locations: hierarchy from site to bin, custom location types, cycle counts, and barcode/QR labels.
  • Reorder points: AI-suggested levels and draft reorders for your approval. It never orders on its own.
  • Paperwork & transfers: standard hand-receipt and property-transfer templates — including specialized government forms when your work requires them — with signatures and chain of custody.
  • Freight visibility: enter or upload shipments, track ETAs, and reconcile what arrived against the order.
Module

AI Fleet Operations Coming Soon

For small and mid-size carriers, brokers, and owner-operators.

  • Billing and POD extraction: read rate confirmations and proof-of-delivery documents and prepare invoices for review.
  • Driver qualification compliance: track expirations and missing items before they become violations.
  • Sourcing: support for finding loads and inbound driver candidates.
  • Maintenance and routing: telematics-informed service reminders and routing support.
  • Settlements: prepare driver and owner-operator settlements for approval.

Fleet Operations is rolling out in stages after the Logistics core. Choose it when you create your account to be notified when it's available.
